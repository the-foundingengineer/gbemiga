Motion /Hurupay

Overview

Produced a product-focused motion video that highlighted HuruPay's cross-border payment experience and digital finance ecosystem. Using interface animations, bold typography, and fast-paced visual storytelling, the video demonstrated how users can move money seamlessly across borders while reinforcing HuruPay's vision of accessible, borderless financial services.
